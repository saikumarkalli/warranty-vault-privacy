# Warranty Vault privacy policy

Effective date: 2026-11-01

Warranty Vault is an Android app for keeping track of product warranties, bills and expiry reminders. This page says what the app stores, where it stores it, and what leaves your phone. The short version: nothing does, unless you send it yourself.

## What this app stores

The app stores the following on your phone, in its own storage area:

- The products you add: name, brand, model, serial number, IMEI, category, purchase date, price, store name, order ID, notes, warranty length or expiry date, and whether reminders are on.
- The documents you attach: photos and PDFs of bills, warranty cards and similar papers. When you attach a file, the app makes its own copy; it does not keep a link to the original.
- Your settings: reminder choices, theme and whether you have finished the first-run screens.

All of this stays on this device. The app has no account, no sign-in and no server.

## No internet, no collection

The app does not declare the Android internet permission, so it cannot connect to a network. You can check this yourself in the permissions list on the app's page in Google Play, or in your phone's system settings.

Because of that:

- The developer collects no data from the app.
- No data is shared with, or sold to, anyone.
- The app contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting code.
- The app does not use Android Auto Backup, so Google does not copy your data to Drive. Your data is not carried to a new phone automatically.

## Permissions the app asks for

- Notifications: to show warranty reminders.
- Camera: only when you choose to photograph a bill or card. The photo is saved as a document inside the app.
- Photos you already have: attached with the Android photo picker, which needs no permission. The app does not scan your gallery and sees only the pictures you choose.

The system also adds a few permissions on behalf of the library that schedules reminders (keeping the phone awake briefly, and starting its work again after a restart). None of them lets the app send your data anywhere.

You can turn any of these off in your phone's settings. The app keeps working without them; reminders and the camera shortcut simply stop.

## Anything you share

You can also share a document, or send a problem report, with Android's share sheet. The app hands the content to whichever app you choose. Nothing is sent unless you pick an app and send it yourself. A problem report contains your phone model, Android version and API level, the app version and build number, the time of the last reminder check, and whatever you type. It is never sent automatically.

## Reminders (notifications)

Reminders are scheduled and shown on the phone itself. They are worked out from the dates you entered: 30 days before expiry, 7 days before and on the day, as you choose in Settings. No server is involved.

## Deleting your data

- Delete a product: it moves to Recently deleted and is erased, with its documents, 30 days later. You can erase it sooner from that screen.
- Delete everything: Settings, Delete all data. This erases every product, document and setting on the phone.
- Uninstalling the app also removes its data.

There is no account, so there is nothing to delete anywhere else. Any shared copy that you made sits outside the app and is yours to delete.

## Children

The app is a general utility and is not directed at children. It collects no information from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this page, this page will change first and the effective date above will move.
